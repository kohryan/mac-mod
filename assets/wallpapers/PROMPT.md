# Wallpaper generation

Tool: built-in ImageGen. Generated for this project on 2026-10-31.

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: real raster desktop wallpaper for a MacBook, wide 16:10 landscape, ideally 2880x1800 or higher.
Primary request: a sophisticated clean minimal Gundam pilot aesthetic. A cinematic photorealistic 3D render of the iconic white RX-78-2 Gundam standing at rest in a vast futuristic orbital hangar at night. The mecha is the main subject in the center, seen as a striking 3/4 full-body or medium-wide mechanical portrait with recognizable white armor, restrained blue and red torso accents, yellow V-fin, subtle cyan eye glow. Beautiful convincing physical materials, detailed engineering, atmospheric depth, refined soft hangar lighting and gentle reflections, deep charcoal/navy scene with tasteful cyan lights.
Composition/framing: desktop wallpaper, uncluttered and spacious, mecha mostly occupies the central 40 percent, quieter darker negative space on the far left and right for separate transparent glass desktop widgets. Sophisticated cinematic composition, not a dashboard screenshot. Nothing is overlaid.
Constraints: actual full-bleed illustrated wallpaper image only. No UI, no widgets, no clocks, no text, no typography, no labels, no decorative HUD frames, no charts, no logos, no watermark. Not vector art, not wireframe, not an SVG-looking schematic. The final image itself must be impressive and useful as the operating-system wallpaper.
